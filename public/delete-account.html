Delete your Tidepop data

Tidepop (the Android app and the website) lets you delete everything we keep about you, yourself, at any time. No email is needed and nothing is kept "just in case".

Delete your account and cloud save

  1. Open Tidepop (the app, or the website) and tap ⚙️ Settings.
  2. In the Account box tap Delete account.
  3. Confirm with your password – or, if you signed in with Google, with the same Google account.

This permanently deletes the account, its cloud save and the link to Google. Progress stored on a device stays on that device until you reset it in Settings.

Delete your online scores

Settings → Leaderboards → Delete my online scores removes your leaderboard name and every score we hold for you.

Delete your usage data

Settings → Send anonymous usage counts → switch it off. This stops the counts, deletes the random install ID on your device and asks our server to erase everything stored under it. (Under-13 players never get an install ID.)

Can't open the app any more?

Install Tidepop again (or open the website) and log in with your username and password, or with Google, then follow the steps above. If you can no longer sign in at all, contact us at the privacy address on the privacy page with your username and we will delete the account for you.
