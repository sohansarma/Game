Privacy & terms

Last updated 20 November 2026. Plain language on purpose – if anything is unclear, the short version is: we collect as little as we can, never sell data, and you can delete what we keep.

What stays on your device

  • Your progress, settings, shells, stickers and your cove are saved in your browser's local storage. Without an account nothing else is needed.
  • The website uses no cookies and no third-party trackers. In the Android app, ads (see "Ads" below) are shown by Google AdMob, which may use the device's advertising ID for adults who agree to it.

Leaderboards (optional, on by default, switch off in Settings)

  • When you post a score we receive the name you chose, a random player ID, and the moves of that game, so the server can replay it and check the score is real.
  • Daily boards are deleted after 30 days and weekly boards after 12 weeks. You can delete all your online scores in Settings.

Accounts (optional)

  • Accounts are for players 13 and older. No email is needed. We store your username, a salted hash of your password (never the password itself) and your game save, so you can play on any device. You can delete the account in Settings, which deletes the save with it.
  • Sign in with Google (optional): instead of a password you can use your Google account. Google tells us only a sign-in ID that belongs to your Google account. We do not receive or keep your email address, name or picture. Google's own privacy policy applies to the sign-in itself, and Google's sign-in script is loaded only when you open the account form.
  • Inside claude.ai, your save is kept privately in your Claude account's area of the game's database instead.

Anonymous usage counts (optional, on by default, switch off in Settings)

  • To know whether the game is fun and where it breaks, the app sends small events such as "a game of Reef Tower started or ended", "the app crashed", "the page loaded slowly" or "an ad was watched". They carry no name, no account, no cookie and we keep no IP address log.
  • Players 13 and older also send a random install ID that stays on your device (never in your save or account). It lets us see how many players come back after 1, 7 and 30 days. We keep events for up to 180 days.
  • Players under 13 never get an install ID: their events only add to anonymous daily totals.
  • Switch it off in Settings → "Send anonymous usage counts". That stops sending, deletes the install ID on your device, and asks the server to delete everything stored under it.

Ads (Android app only)

  • The website shows no ads. The Android app offers optional rewarded ads: you choose to watch a short ad for bonus shells, or to revive a run that ran out of room (once per run). Ads never interrupt a game.
  • Ads are provided by Google AdMob. For players under 13 (and until you've answered the age question) ads are child-directed, limited to a general-audience content rating, not personalised, and use no advertising ID.
  • For players 13 and older we first show Google's consent form where the law requires it (for example in the EEA and UK). Ads are personalised only if you agree; otherwise they are non-personalised. You can change your choice in Settings → Ad choices.

Children

  • Tidepop is for everyone and is not aimed only at children. We ask your age band once, with a neutral question, and we don't store your birthday. Players under 13 get a random friendly name (no custom names), can't publish games in Creator Studio, sign in with Google or create an account, have no install ID in usage counts, and only see child-directed, non-personalised ads. Their progress is saved on their device, and they can move it with a save code.

Community games

  • Games you publish are public with your chosen name. Players who have played a game can rate or report it; reported versions are held until a moderator checks them. Delete your games any time.

Terms

  • Tidepop is free to play. Be kind: no hateful, sexual, violent or personal content in names or community games. We may remove content or names that break this.
  • Don't cheat or attack the service. Scores are verified by replay; forged ones are rejected.
  • Shells, stickers and outfits are for fun and have no cash value.
  • The game is provided as is; we do our best to keep your progress safe, and export/import in Settings lets you keep your own backup.
