# Casual / Puzzle Mobile Games: Market Research (2025–2026)

*Compiled 2026-11-17. Data comes from public summaries of Sensor Tower, AppMagic, company reports and trade press (PocketGamer.biz, MobileGamer.biz, GameDevReports). Third-party tracker estimates differ by tracker and method. AppMagic reports net revenue (after store cut, excluding China Android); Sensor Tower reports gross IAP. Treat every figure as an approximate order of magnitude. Ad revenue is mostly **not** included, which understates ad-monetized games like Block Blast.*

> **Method note:** Direct page fetches to several trade sites were blocked from the research environment, so the figures below come from search-engine summaries of those sources. Where sources conflict, both values are shown. Figures marked "(company)" are self-reported and unverified.

## 3. Fastest-growing genre: block puzzle

- AppMagic names **Block Puzzle the fastest-growing sub-genre of 2025 in both revenue and downloads** ([AppMagic Mobile Market Landscape 2026](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/appmagic-mobile-market-landscape)).
- Block Blast was the #1 downloaded game in **2024 and 2025**, with downloads up 66% YoY. It went from roughly 0 to 300M MAU without a known IP, a story, or a big IAP economy.
- The hybrid-casual puzzle boom covers more than block puzzles. Mechanics like sort, screw/jam and "escape" are the other fast growers. **Sort Puzzle grew 5.6x YoY in Q1 2025**, and Block Puzzle held 71% of hybrid-casual puzzle revenue ([AppMagic Q1 2025 hybrid-casual](https://investgame.net/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/Hybdrid-casual-Games-in-Q1-2025.pdf), [Mobidictum](https://mobidictum.com/sort-block-screw-mechanics-puzzle-games-market/)).
- **Caution:** novelty puzzles like *Arrows* spike and then collapse (MAU roughly halved in 3 months). Block puzzles have stayed on top for more than 2 years, so the format has lasting demand.
- Match-3 is still the revenue king (Royal Match, Candy Crush, Royal Kingdom). It depends on hundreds of hand-made levels, live-ops and IAP economies, which don't fit a free, ad-free indie project.

## 4. Which genres suit "all ages"?

Player base: the average player is ~41 years old, 25–44 is the largest group (49%), and there are more players aged 45–54 than 16–24 ([ESA Global Report 2025](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/esa-global-video-games-report-2025)). Women over-index on mobile and on puzzle and word games ([Business of Apps](https://www.businessofapps.com/?p=97879), [Statista](https://WWW.STATISTA.COM/statistics/1395578/global-daily-mobile-gaming-engagement-puzzle-gender/)).

| Genre | All-ages fit | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Block puzzle (Block Blast / 1010! / Tetris-like)** | ★★★★★ | No language, no reading, no reflex demands (no timer). Understood in 5 seconds. Works for kids through seniors |
| Match-3 | ★★★★☆ | Universal, but needs many levels. Monetization pressure (lives, boosters) can frustrate players |
| 2048 / number merge | ★★★★☆ | Simple, but requires basic numeracy and has a steeper strategy curve |
| Daily word games (Wordle) | ★★★☆☆ | Excellent habit loop, but depends on language and literacy, and localization is hard |
| Sudoku / logic | ★★★☆☆ | Strong with adults and older players, weaker with kids |
| Endless runner (Subway Surfers) | ★★★☆☆ | Huge with kids and teens. Its reflex demands exclude many older players |
| Merge-2 / farm / decor | ★★★☆☆ | Skews toward adult women. Long meta-progression, heavy IAP design |

## 5. Core retention and addictiveness mechanics

Benchmarks: casual puzzle retention runs about **D1 30–40%, D7 10–20%, D30 3–7%**. Mistplay data gives 31.9% / 12.2% / 5.4% ([Segwise](https://segwise.ai/blog/mobile-gaming-app-user-retention-strategies.md)). GameAnalytics reports a cross-genre median D30 of only ~0.7%, with the top 10% at about 4% ([GameAnalytics 2026](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/gameanalytics-mobile-and-pc-game)). Puzzle games retain unusually well.

| Mechanic | How it drives retention | Example | Use in an ethical, ad-free build? |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Short sessions (1–5 min runs)** | "One more game" is easy to justify. Fits commutes and breaks | Block Blast, 2048 | **Yes, core** |
| **Near-miss** | A board that is almost full or a line that is almost cleared reads as "so close", which prompts a replay | Block Blast, Tetris | Yes. It emerges naturally from the design; don't rig it |
| **Combos and streak multipliers** | Clearing several lines, or clearing on consecutive turns, gives escalating score with "Great / Perfect!" feedback, sound and haptics | Block Blast, Candy Crush cascades | **Yes, core** |
| **Variable rewards** | Random piece sets mean every board is different, so payoffs are unpredictable | Block Blast, gacha games | Yes, through random pieces. Avoid loot boxes |
| **Juicy feedback** | Particles, screen shake, satisfying sounds, haptics | All top games | **Yes, cheap and very effective** |
| **Personal best and leaderboards** | Mastery goals plus social comparison: daily, weekly and all-time boards, plus friends | Arcade games, Wordle sharing | **Yes, the headline feature** |
| **Daily challenge / streaks** | Same seeded puzzle for everyone each day, a streak counter and a shareable result grid. Drives D7 and D30 | Wordle (4.2B plays), NYT Connections | **Yes, highly recommended** |
| **Lives and level progression** | Hand-made levels give a feeling of progress. Lives gate sessions and push IAP | Candy Crush, Royal Match | Partly. Levels or an adventure mode can come later; skip lives |
| **Meta-progression / collection** | Decor, stickers, themes unlocked over time | Gardenscapes, Monopoly GO | Light version: unlockable cosmetic themes for milestones |
| **Missions / achievements** | Short-term goals like "clear 3 lines at once" | Most casual games | Yes, cheap to add |
| **Revive after loss (ad-gated)** | A revenue mechanic that exploits near-misses | Block Blast | **No ads.** Replace with one free "undo" or "bomb" per run, or skip it |

## 6. Recommendation

### Build: a Block Blast-style block puzzle (8×8 grid, place 3 pieces, clear rows and columns) as a free, ad-free PWA with leaderboards and a Wordle-style daily challenge

**Why this genre**
1. **Biggest and fastest-growing audience:** #1 downloaded game for 2 years, ~300M MAU (company), and the fastest-growing sub-genre of 2025 (AppMagic).
2. **Truly all ages:** language-free, no timer, learned in seconds, with high skill depth for score chasers.
3. **Ideal for an indie web game:** no level content pipeline (unlike match-3), a small codebase, and it runs smoothly on Canvas or DOM on any phone. Endless arcade scoring makes a **leaderboard meaningful**, which match-3 levels don't.
4. **Ad-free is a real differentiator:** the leading block-puzzle apps are ad-heavy (interstitials and revive ads). "No ads, no lives, no pay-to-win" is a clear pitch, and a PWA install avoids app-store costs.
5. **Proven retention loop:** short sessions, combos, near-misses, random pieces and personal bests already drive the genre's retention. A daily seeded challenge adds the Wordle-style habit loop and fixes the genre's weak D30.

**Recommended feature set (MVP → v1)**
- **Classic endless mode:** 8×8 board, 3 random pieces per round, row and column clears, combo and streak multiplier, game over when no piece fits.
- **Daily challenge:** same seed and piece sequence for everyone (deterministic RNG). One attempt or best-of-N, a daily leaderboard, a streak counter, and a shareable emoji result card.
- **Leaderboards:** daily, weekly and all-time, global and friends. Validate scores server-side by replaying the seeded move log to block cheating.
- **Juice:** clear animations, combo callouts, sounds, haptics (`navigator.vibrate`), and a full-board "Perfect clear" bonus.
- **Accessibility for all ages:** big touch targets, colorblind-safe palette with shape patterns, adjustable piece-drag offset, no time pressure, optional sound.
- **Light meta:** achievements and unlockable color themes. No currency, no lives, no ads.
- **PWA:** offline play (service worker), installable, local save, sync when online.
- **Later options:** an adventure mode with goal levels ("clear 20 gems"), or a 2048 / sort-puzzle side mode if the core sticks.

**Runner-up alternatives**
- *Daily word or logic puzzle (Wordle-like):* great habit loop, but language-bound and skewed toward adults.
- *2048 variant:* simpler, but the genre is saturated and less exciting on a leaderboard.
- *Match-3:* the biggest revenue, but needs too much level content and depends on IAP.
